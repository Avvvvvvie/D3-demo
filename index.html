Visualizations with D3.js

Part 1: Conception

The data that I will visualize comes from my digital diary of 4 Years. I have recently been learning to make an LLM out of it, for which visualizations are a helpful first step to understand the data. For this 3D.js project I visualized three simple things: How much was written over time, how many times a mood was mentioned and how many times were certain words mentioned. (A mood is a tag on each diary entry.)

Part 2: Acquiring and cleaning up the data

The original data comes in folders per year. The data was anonymized with a python script so that it can be shown here. The mood was not anonymized. Then, date, text length and mood were extracted for each file. Finally, all the data was saved as 1 json file so that D3.js can read it in one go.

Part 3: Creating the visualization

Below, the bar chart shows the number of characters written per month. The data was aggregated to each month and the timescale was created in even intervals with the scaleBand function. Hovering on a bar highlights it and shows a tooltip.

The pie chart shows how often a mood was mentioned. Hovering an element highlights it both in the legend and in the pie.

The streamgraph shows the count of certain words per month. Since the word characters were randomized, their occurrence is quite random. There is only a slight correlation to how much was written during that time interval. Most words that are longer than 3 characters only appear once. Hovering shows the counts of all words in that month. Clicking shows all diary entries of that month in which one of these words appear. Using the original dataset, one would be able to see relevant words phasing in and out of my life
